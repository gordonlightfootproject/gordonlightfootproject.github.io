About the group

Songs that have traveled far.

Gordon Lightfoot's songs invite close listening: plainspoken images, patient melodies, and stories that widen as they unfold. The Gordon Lightfoot Project approaches his writing with admiration, affection, and respect.

With multiple lead voices and diverse instrumentation, songs come forward in unique ways. The hope is to let these timeless songs breathe in a live room.

Musicians

Several voices, one shared affection.

The ensemble brings together longtime Chico musicians, multiple lead voices, and a shared care for Lightfoot's songs.
